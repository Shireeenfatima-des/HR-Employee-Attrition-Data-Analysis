# HR-Employee-Attrition-Data-Analysis

## About dataset
The HR Employee Attrition Dataset, sourced from Kaggle, is a popular dataset for exploring workforce analytics and attrition-related factors. It simulates data for a fictional organization and is widely used in HR analytics projects, especially for predicting employee turnover and understanding its driving factors.

### Overview of the project
This project leverages the dataset to gain insights into various aspects of employee behavior, including demographic details, job roles, compensation, satisfaction levels, and performance metrics, offering a comprehensive understanding of the organization's workforce and key elements impacting employee retention.

* By executing SQL queries based on predefined KPIs, the analysis covers the following areas:

### Attrition and Retention
1.	What is the attrition rate across the entire organization?
2.	What is the attrition rate by department?
3.	What percentage of employees left the company within their first year?

### Employee Demographics
1.	What is the average age of employees in each department?
2.	How many male and female employees are in each job role?
3.	What percentage of employees in each marital status group left the company?

### Compensation and Benefits
1.	What is the average monthly income by department?	
2.	How does the stock option level vary across departments?
3.	What is the average salary hike percentage by performance rating?

### Performance and Satisfaction
1.	What is the average job satisfaction level by department?
2.	How many employees with high job satisfaction left the company?
3.	What percentage of employees report a high work-life balance?
	
### Training and Development
1.	What is the average training time by department?
2.	How many employees received no training in the last year?
3.	What is the attrition rate for employees who received no training?

### Experience and Tenure
1.	What is the average tenure by job role?
2.	How many employees have not been promoted in the last 5 years?
3.	What is the average number of years employees have worked with their current manager?

### Travel and Commute
1.	What is the attrition rate by frequency of business travel?
2.	What is the average distance from home by department for employees who left?

Conclusion
The project provides valuable insights into employee behaviors, highlighting critical areas that influence retention and satisfaction. Key takeaways include:
1.	Attrition: Identifying high-turnover departments and demographics can help tailor retention strategies.
2.	Compensation: Addressing income disparities and linking benefits to performance can improve satisfaction.
3.	Training: Ensuring employees receive adequate training supports both retention and professional growth.
4.	Satisfaction: Understanding job satisfaction levels and work-life balance helps in creating a better workplace culture.
5.	Commute and Travel: Mitigating the effects of long commutes or excessive travel can reduce attrition rates.
By addressing these insights, the organization can enhance employee engagement, reduce turnover, and optimize workforce management strategies.
